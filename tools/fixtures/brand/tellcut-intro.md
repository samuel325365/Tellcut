Tellcut is a video editor that cuts your film for you. We all film everything — every trip, every first, every moment worth keeping — and then the clips sit on our phones. With Tellcut you say one sentence, like "make a 30-second film of our Lisbon trip", and you get a first cut. It watches every clip, picks the best shots, adds captions and music on the beat, makes it vertical, landscape or square, and checks its own work before you see it. It comes with 130+ built-in effects and sounds, speaks three languages (English, 中文, 日本語), and your files stay on your computer. Tell it. It cuts. github.com/samuel325365/Tellcut
