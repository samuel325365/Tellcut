# Skit / POV (`skit`)

Chinese name: 短劇／POV. Category: Entertainment & drama.

- Definition: One situation, one twist, often one person playing several roles.
- Goal: shares, following the series
- Ratios: 9:16 (main)
- Length: 15–90 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. Open with a "POV: …" card
2. A twist at the end
3. When one person plays several roles, tell them apart by costume or props

## Extras

- Recurring characters, made into a series
- SFX to land the jokes

## Pitfalls

- Too much set-up, the twist comes too late

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `pov` POV scenario: the 30-second scenario format the sources list
2. `telop` Variety telop: telop and SFX sharpen the jokes
3. `pastel` Symmetry & pastel (Wes Anderson trend): symmetry and pastels turn the everyday into a fairy tale
