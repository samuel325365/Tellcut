# Data visualization (`dataviz`)

Chinese name: 數據視覺化. Category: Education.

- Definition: Answer one question with animated charts, e.g. a bar-chart race or a changing map.
- Goal: shares, completion rate
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16, 1:1
- Length: 30 s–5 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. One clear question
2. Rolling numbers and a timeline
3. Animated rank changes

## Extras

- Notes on key years (e.g. 2020 pandemic)
- Music mood following the data
- End on a conclusion frame

## Pitfalls

- Wrong data; this audience catches errors
- Too many things moving at once to read

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `essay` Video essay (Vox / Johnny Harris): charts and maps are the core of a video essay
2. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: rolling numbers and years are kinetic type
3. `flatanim` Flat animated explainer: illustrations fill in the story behind the data

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Horizontal bar charts and maps work best.
- 9:16: Switch to a vertical ranked list, year in big type on top.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
