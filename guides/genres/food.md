# Food (review / recipe) (`food`)

Chinese name: 美食. Category: Real life.

- Definition: A restaurant visit or a recipe; it has to look delicious.
- Goal: saves, visiting the place or cooking it
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9, 1:1
- Length: 20 s–10 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Open on a close-up of the finished dish: the bite, the cheese pull, the pour
2. Recipe: top-down shots, numbered steps
3. Sounds of eating or cooking

## Extras

- Rating card
- Bleep or cover the shop name as a hook
- Slow motion
- Menu price list

## Pitfalls

- Bad light makes the food look unappetising

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: sources name food as a subject that suits the cinematic look
2. `hands` Hands-only ASMR: top-down recipe, only hands and cooking sounds
3. `telop` Variety telop: telop for review quips and scores
4. `ugcnative` Native phone-shot UGC: feels like a friend's recommendation, very credible

## Layout by ratio

- 9:16: A top-down plate fills the vertical frame neatly.
- 16:9: Long recipe: split steps left/right, ingredients left, action right.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
