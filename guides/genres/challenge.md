# Challenge (`challenge`)

Chinese name: 挑戰. Category: Entertainment & drama.

- Definition: Set a rule and see whether it can be done.
- Goal: completion rate (waiting for the result)
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9
- Length: 30 s–20 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. Explain the rules fast
2. A timer or counter
3. A clear result

## Extras

- A penalty for failing
- Invite viewers to try it
- Comic-style counter stickers

## Pitfalls

- Dangerous or against platform rules

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `retention` Retention editing: challenges are where MrBeast-style editing was born
2. `telop` Variety telop: mark counts, failures and reactions with telop
3. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: slow down at the moment of success
