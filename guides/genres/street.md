# Street interview (`street`)

Chinese name: 街訪. Category: Entertainment & drama.

- Definition: Ask strangers on the street the same question and keep the funniest answers.
- Goal: comment discussion
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9
- Length: 30 s–10 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. A question card
2. The funniest answer first
3. A branded microphone

## Extras

- A tally: "7 out of 10 people said…"
- Close-ups of interviewees' reactions

## Pitfalls

- No consent from the interviewees

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `telop` Variety telop: telop blows up the funny answers
2. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: answers must be readable word by word
3. `retention` Retention editing: keep only the best answers
