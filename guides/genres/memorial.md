# Memorial film (`memorial`)

Chinese name: 追思紀念影片. Category: Live events & keepsakes.

- Definition: Played at a funeral or memorial, looking back on a life.
- Goal: help family and friends say goodbye
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 4:3
- Length: 3–8 min; mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Slow pace, soft transitions
2. Sections for the stages of life
3. Restrained music

## Extras

- Recordings or clips of the person while alive
- Handwriting or family letters

## Pitfalls

- Too many effects, too flashy
- The family not shown it first

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `docreal` Documentary realism: restrained grade and pace
2. `retro` Retro digicam / DV: the period feel of old photos
3. `silentvlog` Silent vlog: captions instead of narration, telling it quietly
