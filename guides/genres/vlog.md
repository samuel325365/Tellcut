# Vlog / day in my life (`vlog`)

Chinese name: Vlog／我的一天. Category: Real life.

- Definition: A stretch of time strung together from the main character's point of view; viewers watch the person and the lifestyle.
- Goal: follows, attachment to the person
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 1–20 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. The main character's voice: narration or inner-voice captions
2. At least one highlight moment

## Extras

- Jump cuts to remove dead time
- Emoji in captions
- Mini map
- A fixed opening and closing ritual

## Pitfalls

- A diary of events with no point

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `silentvlog` Silent vlog: YouTube's trend report: no talking, captions tell it, viewers come for calm
2. `cleanbroll` Polished talking head + B-roll: for a talking vlog, B-roll carries the pace
3. `retro` Retro digicam / DV: the digicam trend suits casual life records
