# Short film (`shortfilm`)

Chinese name: 微電影／短片. Category: Entertainment & drama.

- Definition: A fully scripted narrative piece; acting, cinematography and sound design matter.
- Goal: festivals, brand goodwill, portfolio
- Ratios: 21:9 (main), 16:9
- Length: 3–30 min; mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. A complete three-act structure
2. One consistent visual style and grade
3. Sound design (ambience, score, dialogue)

## Extras

- Cinematic black bars
- Original score
- Trailer and poster

## Pitfalls

- Poor audio recording
- Dragging pace

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `docreal` Documentary realism: cinematic camera, one grade, few captions
2. `pastel` Symmetry & pastel (Wes Anderson trend): the signature approach for stylised storytelling
3. `retro` Retro digicam / DV: memory and period subjects
