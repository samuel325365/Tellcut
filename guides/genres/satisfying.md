# Satisfying / process timelapse (`satisfying`)

Chinese name: 療癒／過程縮時. Category: Sensory & soothing.

- Definition: Show a whole process: cleaning, restoring, crafting, assembling.
- Goal: completion rate, rewatches
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9, 1:1
- Length: 15 s–10 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. A continuous process, never skipping the key step
2. A satisfying result
3. Natural sound

## Extras

- Speed curve: fast through dull parts, slow on the good ones
- Physics or particle effects simulated in code

## Pitfalls

- Skipping the very step viewers most want to see

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `hands` Hands-only ASMR: the restoration or craft process is the content
2. `flatlay` Aesthetic flat-lay: the soothing feel of items laid out one by one
3. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: a polished look for the reveal
