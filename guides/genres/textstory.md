# Text story + gameplay (`textstory`)

Chinese name: 文字故事加遊戲畫面. Category: Templates & interaction.

- Definition: Story captions and voice on the top half, constantly moving gameplay on the bottom half.
- Goal: completion rate, following the series
- Ratios: 9:16 (main)
- Length: 45 s–3 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. TTS voice
2. Word-by-word highlighted captions
3. A constantly moving background in the lower half
4. Episodes

## Extras

- Character avatars and speech bubbles

## Pitfalls

- Platforms are limiting mass-produced repetitive content; every video needs original elements

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `splitstory` Story over gameplay: the standard format of this genre
2. `brainrot` Brainrot stack: stack one more layer for extra stimulation
3. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: word-by-word highlighted captions
