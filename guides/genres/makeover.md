# Before & after (`makeover`)

Chinese name: 改造前後. Category: Real life.

- Definition: Show the bad starting point first, reveal the result at the end.
- Goal: completion rate (viewers wait for the result)
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9, 1:1
- Length: 20 s–10 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Open with the "before" and tease the "after"
2. Timelapse of the process
3. Reveal from the same angle, ideally as a draggable comparison

## Extras

- Time and money spent, as numbers
- A failure along the way
- Countdown to the reveal

## Pitfalls

- Before and after from different angles, so the comparison fails

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `hands` Hands-only ASMR: restoration and makeovers are the classic "boring work" ASMR subjects
2. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: cinematic look for the reveal
3. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: speed ramps compress a long process
