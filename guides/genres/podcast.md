# Podcast & interview clip (`podcast`)

Chinese name: Podcast／訪談切片. Category: Repurposing.

- Definition: A kind of long-form clip: a conversation between two or more people; viewers must always know who is talking.
- Goal: clicks to the full episode, subscriptions
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9, 1:1
- Length: 30 s–3 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Several camera angles, switching with whoever is speaking
2. Captions coloured per speaker
3. Cold open on the best line

## Extras

- Glow outline or slight zoom on the active speaker
- Listener reaction shots: laughing, nodding
- Audio waveform animation (especially for audio-only)
- A third small window with a picture about the topic
- Show logo watermark

## Pitfalls

- Cutting off heads when cropping 16:9 to 9:16
- Captions turning into a mess when two people talk at once
- Camera switching faster than the speech

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `split` Split / stacked layout: the standard layout for multi-person talk; viewers always know who is speaking
2. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: with the sound off, the captions are the content
3. `telop` Variety telop: chat shows need telop to make the jokes land

## Layout by ratio

- 9:16: Two people stacked (half each), or the main speaker large plus one small window.
- 16:9: Two or three windows side by side; the main speaker's larger, the others smaller beside it.
- 1:1: Title bar on top, two windows side by side in the middle, captions below.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
