# ASMR (`asmr`)

Chinese name: ASMR. Category: Sensory & soothing.

- Definition: Sound is the subject, bringing relaxation or tingles.
- Goal: long watch time, replays
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9
- Length: 30 s–1 h; short (under 60 s), long (over 5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. High-quality audio
2. No background music, or very quiet
3. Shot up close

## Extras

- Slow motion
- Left/right stereo space

## Pitfalls

- Background noise
- A sudden loud sound

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `hands` Hands-only ASMR: sources: top-down, no music, only the clean sound of the work
2. `silentvlog` Silent vlog: everyday-life ASMR
3. `flatlay` Aesthetic flat-lay: object ASMR in aesthetic top-down shots
