# Pets (`pet`)

Chinese name: 寵物. Category: Real life.

- Definition: Animal expressions and behaviour, often with an "inner voice" written for them.
- Goal: shares, follows
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 1:1
- Length: 10–45 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: not needed
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. Close-ups of expressions
2. Well-timed cuts

## Extras

- Voice-over or inner-voice captions
- Meme SFX
- Slow motion

## Pitfalls

- Footage too long with no standout moment

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `telop` Variety telop: writing a pet's inner thoughts is what telop does best
2. `pov` POV scenario: tell the story from the pet's point of view
3. `photodump` Photo dump: everyday photos on the beat

- Every ratio: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".
