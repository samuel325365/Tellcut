# Event opener (`opener`)

Chinese name: 活動開場影片. Category: Live events & keepsakes.

- Definition: Played as an event begins, to lift the room.
- Goal: atmosphere in the room
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 21:9
- Length: 30 s–2 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Music with a clear build and drop
2. The ending hands over to the host walking on stage

## Extras

- A countdown
- Big kinetic type
- Special ratios for ultra-wide or multi-screen setups

## Pitfalls

- Venue screen ratio does not match the export
- Volume not tested in the venue beforehand

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: big kinetic type is the core of an opener
2. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: a rhythm that builds to a climax
3. `audioreactive` Audio-reactive loop: a key visual pulsing with the music

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Ordinary projection screens and LED.
- 21:9: Wide LED walls; ask for the exact resolution first.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
