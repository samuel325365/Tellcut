# Quiz (`quiz`)

Chinese name: 測驗互動. Category: Templates & interaction.

- Definition: Question, countdown, reveal: viewers answer as they watch.
- Goal: comments, completion rate
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9
- Length: 30 s–10 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Clear questions
2. A countdown timer with ticking
3. Reveal animation and SFX
4. "How many did you get? Tell me in the comments"

## Extras

- Rising difficulty
- Score keeping
- A template with a swappable question bank

## Pitfalls

- Countdown too short or too long

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: questions, countdowns and reveals are all text animation
2. `retention` Retention editing: no dead air between questions
3. `flatanim` Flat animated explainer: illustrated questions
