# Gameplay / sports highlight (`highlight`)

Chinese name: 精華高光. Category: Repurposing.

- Definition: One key moment, plus the build-up and replays that make it hit.
- Goal: completion rate, rewatches
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 10–60 s (compilations up to 10 min); short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. 3–5 s of build-up so viewers know the situation
2. The key moment on a music downbeat or an SFX
3. Slow-motion replay

## Extras

- Freeze just before the moment, mark it with red circles and arrows
- Score, health bar and other HUD info
- Multi-angle replay
- The crowd or commentator erupting

## Pitfalls

- Build-up too long
- Broadcast sports footage, usually the strictest copyright

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: the key moment on a downbeat with speed ramps around it amplifies the payoff best
2. `retention` Retention editing: a compilation must always have something happening
3. `telop` Variety telop: point out details viewers might miss

## Layout by ratio

- 9:16: A moving crop that follows the action, not fixed on the centre.
- 16:9: Compilation form, segments separated by score or round cards.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
