# Kinetic typography (`kinetic`)

Chinese name: 動態文字／語錄. Category: Templates & interaction.

- Definition: The text itself is the star, appearing with the rhythm of music or speech.
- Goal: shares, saves
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 1:1, 16:9
- Length: 10–60 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Text appears on the beat
2. Contrast in weight and size
3. One line per frame

## Extras

- Key words change colour or move
- Subtle motion in the background

## Pitfalls

- Too many or too fancy fonts

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: the text is the star
2. `audioreactive` Audio-reactive loop: text and background change with the music
3. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: text over cinematic B-roll
