# Long-form clipping (`clip`)

Chinese name: 長片切片. Category: Repurposing.

- Definition: Pick the most gripping 30–90 s of a long video, to send viewers back to the original.
- Goal: clicks back to the original, new followers
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 1:1, 16:9
- Length: 20–90 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Cold open: the strongest line in the very first second
2. A title bar that stays the whole video
3. Hook gap: stop right before the answer, or bleep the key word, cover the mouth with a sticker and show the caption as ███
4. Burned-in captions
5. Call-to-action card: "watch the full version"

## Extras

- Punch in on the key point
- Colour or enlarge key words with a pop SFX
- B-roll: photos of the people and things mentioned
- Progress bar
- Pinned comment with the link to the original

## Pitfalls

- Hiding too much or an overblown title: viewers feel cheated
- Clipping someone else's content without permission
- Captions covered by the platform UI

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: the most common captions for talking clips; the key line gets hammered in word by word
2. `telop` Variety telop: familiar to Chinese-speaking viewers; boosts quips and jokes
3. `brainrot` Brainrot stack: holds attention when dense, but easily looks cheap

## Layout by ratio

- 9:16: Face in the upper half, title bar just below the top safe edge, captions in the middle.
- 16:9: Used as a trailer for the long video or a social post: title top left, captions at the bottom.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
