# Product commercial (`tvc`)

Chinese name: 產品廣告. Category: Business & brand.

- Definition: Make people remember one product and one selling point in very little time.
- Goal: recall, purchases
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16, 1:1, 4:5
- Length: 6, 15 or 30 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Only one selling point
2. Product close-ups
3. Call to action in the last 3 s

## Extras

- A 6-s cut-down
- The same film exported in several ratios

## Pitfalls

- Too many selling points; nothing sticks

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: 6–15 s product shots on the beat
2. `flatlay` Aesthetic flat-lay: the mainstream composition for product shots
3. `ugcnative` Native phone-shot UGC: the social-ad version

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: TV and YouTube pre-roll.
- 4:5: The most common feed ad.
- 9:16: Stories and short-video ads; the brand must appear in the first 3 s.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
