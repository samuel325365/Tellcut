# Ambient loop (`ambient`)

Chinese name: 氛圍循環. Category: Sensory & soothing.

- Definition: Picture plus music you can leave running, e.g. lo-fi, a rainy window.
- Goal: long play time
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 10 s loop – several hours; short (under 60 s), long (over 5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Seamless head and tail, loops forever
2. Steady music
3. Little information on screen

## Extras

- Slowly changing elements: time of day, weather
- Small character movements

## Pitfalls

- A visible loop seam

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `audioreactive` Audio-reactive loop: sources: suits autoplaying loop content
2. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: atmospheric scenery
3. `retro` Retro digicam / DV: nostalgic atmosphere
