# Highlight montage (`montage`)

Chinese name: 精華剪輯配 BGM. Category: Real life.

- Definition: Condense an experience (wedding, trip, event) with music driving the emotion.
- Goal: shares, moving the people in it
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16, 21:9
- Length: 30 s–4 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. The right song
2. Cuts on the beat
3. An emotional arc (set-up, build, turn, resolution)
4. The most beautiful shot first
5. One consistent grade

## Extras

- Let the music give way to live sound: laughter, vows
- Speed changes: alternate slow motion and fast forward
- Date/place cards, freeze into a photo
- Chapters

## Pitfalls

- Every shot the same length
- Only music, no human voice at all

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: one grade throughout gives the most "highlight film" feel
2. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: speed ramps to the music make the emotional swings clearest
3. `photodump` Photo dump: for lots of photos and little video
4. `retro` Retro digicam / DV: for a nostalgic, period feel

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: The full cinematic version; can add bars to make it 21:9.
- 9:16: Social version takes only 30–45 s; keep the subject centred.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
