# Stream highlight (`stream`)

Chinese name: 直播切片. Category: Repurposing.

- Definition: Cut one event out of a live stream, with just enough lead-up that people who missed the stream get it.
- Goal: traffic to the stream channel, follows
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9
- Length: 20 s–3 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. The streamer's face cam and the main screen both on screen
2. The moment of the event is unmistakable

## Extras

- Chat messages flying across (danmaku)
- Donation / subscription alert animations
- "Stream time 02:13:45" marker
- Channel name and stream slot

## Pitfalls

- No lead-up, so viewers miss the joke
- Small game text unreadable on a phone

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `split` Split / stacked layout: face cam stacked over gameplay is the stream-clip standard
2. `telop` Variety telop: stream reactions and in-jokes need telop to point them out
3. `retention` Retention editing: highlights should be fast with no dead air

## Layout by ratio

- 9:16: Face cam on top (about 1/3), below it the important area of the game screen, cropped.
- 16:9: Keep the original stream layout; just add a title and captions.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
