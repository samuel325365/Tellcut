# Travel (`travel`)

Chinese name: 旅遊. Category: Real life.

- Definition: Take viewers to a place: informative (a guide) or atmospheric (cinematic).
- Goal: saves, shares with travel companions
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 30 s–20 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. The most spectacular shot first
2. Transitions that feel like movement

## Extras

- Animated route map
- Cost and transport info cards
- First-person walking view
- Ambient sound
- Game-style packaging (e.g. Pokémon-style)

## Pitfalls

- Only scenery, no information (unless deliberately atmospheric)

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: sources tie it directly to solo-travel creators
2. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: movement and transitions on the beat
3. `pastel` Symmetry & pastel (Wes Anderson trend): the Wes Anderson trend began with a commute video
4. `photodump` Photo dump: a quick photo recap of the itinerary

## Layout by ratio

- Every ratio: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".
