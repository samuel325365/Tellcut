# UGC ad (`ugc`)

Chinese name: 素人推薦式廣告. Category: Business & brand.

- Definition: A product recommended in an ordinary person's voice, looking like sharing, not advertising.
- Goal: clicks, purchases
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 4:5, 1:1
- Length: 15–60 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. Open on the pain point
2. Actually using the product
3. Proof it works
4. Call to action

## Extras

- Captions marking key points
- Comparison with other products
- Real facial reactions

## Pitfalls

- Looks too much like an ad
- Sponsorship not disclosed

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `ugcnative` Native phone-shot UGC: sources: the ad libraries of beauty, wellness and e-commerce brands look exactly like this
2. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: pain points and results need word-by-word emphasis
3. `pov` POV scenario: open on a scenario: "Is your skin…?"
