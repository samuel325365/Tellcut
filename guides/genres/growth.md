# Growth & love story (`growth`)

Chinese name: 成長／愛情故事影片. Category: Live events & keepsakes.

- Definition: Played at a wedding banquet: the couple's photos from childhood on tell how they met.
- Goal: emotion and laughter in the room
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 4:3
- Length: 3–6 min; mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Two timelines that finally meet
2. Motion on old photos (push-ins, pans)
3. A song whose lyrics carry the emotion

## Extras

- Old photos kept at 4:3 in a frame
- Short wishes from family and friends
- End on photos from the wedding day

## Pitfalls

- Too many photos, each on screen too briefly
- Low-resolution photos not restored

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `photodump` Photo dump: a photo-led recap on the beat
2. `retro` Retro digicam / DV: date stamps and grain strengthen the sense of era
3. `docreal` Documentary realism: slow, gentle storytelling
