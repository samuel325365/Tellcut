# Top-N list (`listicle`)

Chinese name: 排行清單. Category: Education.

- Definition: "5 things…" counting down to number one; fixed structure, easy to template.
- Goal: completion rate, comments
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9, 1:1
- Length: 30 s–8 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Big countdown numbers
2. Every item the same length and rhythm
3. Number one is the strongest or has a twist

## Extras

- Progress hint "3/5"
- Tease at the start ("you won't guess number one") without revealing it
- Invite viewers to post their own ranking

## Pitfalls

- A long intro: viewers swipe away before the first item

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `retention` Retention editing: each item must switch fast; never make people wait
2. `greenscreen` Greenscreen explain: the presenter stands in front of each item's picture
3. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: the faceless version uses big numbers and animated text

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Long-form can spend 1–2 min per item, with chapter markers.
- 9:16: 5–10 s per item, switching fast with numbered transitions.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
