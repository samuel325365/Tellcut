# Myth busting (`myth`)

Chinese name: 冷知識／迷思破解. Category: Education.

- Definition: Start from a wrong common belief, then flip it with evidence.
- Goal: shares, arguments in the comments
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 1:1
- Length: 20–60 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: optional
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Open with "You think… but actually…"

## Extras

- ❌ and ✅ marks
- A "busted" SFX at the flip
- Source card

## Pitfalls

- Overblown title, but the content does not really flip anything

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `greenscreen` Greenscreen explain: holding up screenshots of the evidence is the most direct debunk
2. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: "you think… actually…" talking needs word-by-word emphasis
3. `doodle` Whiteboard / stick figure: hand-drawn diagrams when faceless
