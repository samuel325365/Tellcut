# Movie / series recap (`recap`)

Chinese name: 影視解說. Category: Repurposing.

- Definition: Narration-led: compress the plot of a film or a season into a few minutes.
- Goal: completion rate, following the series
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 3–15 min (short version 60 s); mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Open with the verdict: "The most brutal thing about this film is…"
2. Steady narration pace, with captions
3. Plot logic that people who have not seen it can follow

## Extras

- Character name tags, relationship map
- Rating card
- "Revealed next episode" to make a series

## Pitfalls

- The highest copyright risk of all genres
- Just retelling the plot with no opinion

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `essay` Video essay (Vox / Johnny Harris): narration up front with lower-third titles explains a plot best
2. `docreal` Documentary realism: the film footage already looks good; use less text
3. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: the vertical short version needs captions to work muted

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Show the film frame in full; a relationship map can sit at the side.
- 9:16: Film in the middle band, title above, captions below; fill the rest with a blurred background.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
