# Animated explainer (`explainer`)

Chinese name: 圖文／動畫解說. Category: Education.

- Definition: One video explains one concept clearly, with graphics and animation instead of filming.
- Goal: saves, shares
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9
- Length: 45 s–10 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Open with a question
2. One video, one idea
3. Voice and captions in sync
4. The picture changes every 2–3 s
5. Visual metaphors for abstract ideas

## Extras

- A recurring character or mascot
- Animated icons and charts
- Chapter progress
- A one-sentence summary at the end

## Pitfalls

- Too much text on screen
- Trying to explain three things in one video

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `flatanim` Flat animated explainer: strips details to the core mechanism; the most recognisable explainer look
2. `doodle` Whiteboard / stick figure: cheap to make, suits light concepts
3. `essay` Video essay (Vox / Johnny Harris): carries more weight for history and social topics
4. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: for a short concept the text alone can hold the frame

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Diagram on the left, key text on the right, or full-frame animation with captions below.
- 9:16: Diagram in the upper half, captions lower middle, one element at a time.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
