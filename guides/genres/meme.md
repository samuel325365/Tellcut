# Meme / trend (`meme`)

Chinese name: 迷因／跟風. Category: Entertainment & drama.

- Definition: Take a trending sound or template and make your own version.
- Goal: quick reach
- Ratios: 9:16 (main)
- Length: 5–30 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Localise the adaptation

## Extras

- Tie in your own recurring character

## Pitfalls

- Joining too late, after the trend is over

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: most trend templates are beat-synced speed ramps
2. `brainrot` Brainrot stack: the high-stimulation editing of meme culture
3. `photodump` Photo dump: photo versions of trend templates
