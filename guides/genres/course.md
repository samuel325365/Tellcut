# Course & lecture (`course`)

Chinese name: 線上課程／長教學. Category: Education.

- Definition: Teach a whole topic systematically, usually as part of a series.
- Goal: course completion, paid conversion
- Ratios: 16:9 (main)
- Length: 10–60 min; long (over 5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Say up front what the lesson lets you do
2. Chapters and timestamps
3. Presenter and slides or screen both clearly visible
4. Clean audio

## Extras

- A recap card at the end of each chapter
- Downloadable handout
- Pause points for exercises

## Pitfalls

- Slide text too small
- Slips and dead air not cut

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `cleanbroll` Polished talking head + B-roll: long teaching needs B-roll and graphics to keep attention
2. `greenscreen` Greenscreen explain: slides as the background, presenter beside them
3. `statichead` Talking-head static: when the presenter is the draw, clean is best

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Slides as the main picture, presenter in a small box bottom right; cut to the presenter full-frame for key points.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
