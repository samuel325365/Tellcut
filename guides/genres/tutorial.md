# How-to / screen tutorial (`tutorial`)

Chinese name: 操作教學. Category: Education.

- Definition: Teach the viewer to do one concrete thing, usually a screen recording or hands doing it.
- Goal: saves, search traffic
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 30 s–20 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Show the result first
2. Numbered steps
3. Enlarge the cursor and the click points

## Extras

- Waits at 2× speed
- Before/after
- Chapter timestamps (long-form)
- "Save this for later" reminder

## Pitfalls

- Screen text too small on a phone
- Talking for ages before doing anything

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `greenscreen` Greenscreen explain: presenter over the screen, explaining while doing
2. `hands` Hands-only ASMR: physical tasks (cooking, crafts) are clearest with just the hands
3. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: talking-style tips

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Full screen recording with zoomed-in close-ups.
- 9:16: Only the zoomed working area; never shrink the whole screen into it.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
