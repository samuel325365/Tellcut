# Brand film (`brand`)

Chinese name: 品牌形象片. Category: Business & brand.

- Definition: Tell a story about people and values, with restrained branding.
- Goal: brand goodwill, trust
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 21:9, 9:16
- Length: 1–5 min; mid (1–5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. A person → struggle → turn story
2. Footage with real quality
3. Restrained branding

## Extras

- Cinematic black bars
- Original score
- A 15-s social cut

## Pitfalls

- The logo on screen from start to finish
- Only empty scenery, no people

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `docreal` Documentary realism: brand stories need high-quality footage to carry them
2. `cinematic` Phone-first cinematic: the cinematic look on a limited budget
3. `pastel` Symmetry & pastel (Wes Anderson trend): stylised brands
