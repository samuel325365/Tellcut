# Display loop (`loopwall`)

Chinese name: 展場／電視牆循環. Category: Live events & keepsakes.

- Definition: Replayed endlessly at a booth, shop or lobby; people pass by and leave at any moment.
- Goal: make passers-by stop
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16, 21:9
- Length: 15 s–3 min loop; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Understandable without sound
2. Seamless loop
3. Makes sense starting from any second
4. Brand or product clearly shown

## Extras

- 9:16 for portrait screens
- Big type, high contrast

## Pitfalls

- Needs sound to make sense
- Text too small to read from a distance

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `audioreactive` Audio-reactive loop: sources: suits autoplaying loops
2. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: big type that reads without sound
3. `flatlay` Aesthetic flat-lay: clean compositions for product display
