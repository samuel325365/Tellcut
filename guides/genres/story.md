# Narrated storytelling (`story`)

Chinese name: 說書／故事. Category: Education.

- Definition: Tell a history, mystery or urban-legend story in voice-over; no face needed.
- Goal: completion rate, following the series
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 1–30 min; mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: not needed
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. A suspense opening
2. A calm, steady narrating voice
3. Atmospheric music and SFX
4. Slow push-ins on images; the picture keeps changing

## Extras

- Serialised episodes
- Maps and date labels
- Old newspapers, documents and other prop shots

## Pitfalls

- One image held too long
- A story with no turn

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `essay` Video essay (Vox / Johnny Harris): narration, maps, archive material: the safest way to tell a story
2. `splitstory` Story over gameplay: the mainstream format for short stories
3. `doodle` Whiteboard / stick figure: sources say hand drawing suits personal stories

- Every ratio: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".
