# Talking head (`talking`)

Chinese name: 個人 IP 口播. Category: Business & brand.

- Definition: Opinions spoken to camera; viewers follow the person.
- Goal: follows, trust, traffic
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9
- Length: 30 s–15 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. The opinion in the very first sentence
2. Jump cuts removing breaths and pauses
3. Big captions, key words coloured
4. Face in the middle, slightly high

## Extras

- B-roll
- Punch in on key points
- SFX
- A recurring catchphrase opening

## Pitfalls

- Sentences too long
- No visual change in the whole video

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: the mainstream approach for talking shorts
2. `statichead` Talking-head static: sources: when viewers care about the opinion rather than production, no cuts works
3. `cleanbroll` Polished talking head + B-roll: the long-form talking version
