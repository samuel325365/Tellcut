# Outfit / GRWM transition (`outfit`)

Chinese name: 穿搭／變裝. Category: Real life.

- Definition: Get ready with me or a one-second outfit change; wins on transitions and the beat.
- Goal: shares, product clicks
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 4:5
- Length: 10–45 s; short (under 60 s)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. Transitions on the beat: hand over the lens, a jump, a spin

## Extras

- Brand and price tags
- Before/after
- Same pose aligned across the transition

## Pitfalls

- Transitions off the beat ruin the whole video

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: outfit-change transitions must land on the beat
2. `flatlay` Aesthetic flat-lay: sources list outfit flat-lays as a signature subject
3. `retro` Retro digicam / DV: digicam-style casual outfit shots
