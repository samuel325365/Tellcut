# Year in review (`yearrecap`)

Chinese name: 年度回顧. Category: Live events & keepsakes.

- Definition: Look back on the key moments of a year (personal, team, company).
- Goal: togetherness, shares
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 1–5 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Music with an emotional arc

## Extras

- Rolling number animations
- Close on a photo wall
- Thank-you credits

## Pitfalls

- It turns into a photo slideshow with no rhythm

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `photodump` Photo dump: a year of photos recapped on the beat
2. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: key numbers rolling
3. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: highlights on the beat

## Layout by ratio

- Every ratio: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
