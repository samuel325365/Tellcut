# Reaction & commentary (`react`)

Chinese name: 反應／評論. Category: Repurposing.

- Definition: The original video fills the main frame; you react and comment from a corner.
- Goal: comments, follows
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 1–20 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: needed (a real person on camera or performing)
- Production: relies mainly on filming or performance

## Must-haves

1. Picture-in-picture: the source is the subject, your face in a small box
2. Pause the source to insert commentary (this is what "adds original content")
3. End with an overall verdict

## Extras

- Freeze the source and circle the key point
- Add information the source did not cover

## Pitfalls

- Faces without commentary: low value and weak on copyright

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `pip` Reaction / duet: the basic reaction layout
2. `greenscreen` Greenscreen explain: you stand in front of the source; feels more like an explanation
3. `telop` Variety telop: turn your quips into telop

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Source takes about 70%, face box bottom right.
- 9:16: Source on top (16:9 shown in full), your face below.

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
