# News explainer (`news`)

Chinese name: 時事快評. Category: Repurposing.

- Definition: Sum up an event quickly after it happens, with your own take.
- Goal: shares, fast traffic
- Ratios: 9:16 (main), 16:9, 1:1
- Length: 45 s–5 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: can be made without filming

## Must-haves

1. Organised as "3 key points"
2. A clear opinion

## Extras

- News screenshots as cards
- Event timeline
- Key numbers enlarged

## Pitfalls

- Posting before verifying
- The topic dates fast, so little long-term traffic

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `greenscreen` Greenscreen explain: the sources name it for news explainers: talking over material you did not shoot
2. `essay` Video essay (Vox / Johnny Harris): switch to video essay when you need maps or a timeline
3. `hormozi` Hormozi-style captions: captions for a talking-style take

## Layout by ratio

- 1:1: Works as a feed-friendly "explained in cards" format.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
