# Unboxing & review (`review`)

Chinese name: 開箱評測. Category: Business & brand.

- Definition: Unbox, test, give pros and cons and who it is for.
- Goal: trust, affiliate clicks
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16
- Length: 45 s–20 min; short (under 60 s), mid (1–5 min), long (over 5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Open with the verdict or who it is for
2. Real test footage
3. A pros/cons list

## Extras

- Side-by-side with a competitor
- Rating card
- Chapter timestamps

## Pitfalls

- Unboxing with no testing
- Sponsorship not disclosed

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `cleanbroll` Polished talking head + B-roll: product close-ups and test B-roll carry a review
2. `flatlay` Aesthetic flat-lay: sources list unboxing and desks as signature subjects
3. `ugcnative` Native phone-shot UGC: short unboxings in an ordinary person's voice

## Layout by ratio

- 16:9: Best for comparing two products side by side.
- Other ratios: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".

Safe area for every ratio: README.md "Ratios".
