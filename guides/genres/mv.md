# Music video (`mv`)

Chinese name: 音樂 MV. Category: Entertainment & drama.

- Definition: The picture follows one song entirely, in service of the song.
- Goal: plays of the song
- Ratios: 16:9 (main), 9:16, 21:9
- Length: 2–5 min; mid (1–5 min)
- Face: optional
- Production: footage plus packaging made in code

## Must-haves

1. Cuts follow the beat and the song sections
2. Verses and choruses look clearly different

## Extras

- Lyric captions (lyric video)
- A separate 9:16 chorus clip for social

## Pitfalls

- An unlicensed song

## Editing styles

In order of fit; traits in README.md "Editing styles".

1. `velocity` Velocity / beat sync: the picture follows the beat
2. `kinetic` Kinetic typography: a lyric video is kinetic type
3. `audioreactive` Audio-reactive loop: visualise the music, make loopable clips

- Every ratio: no genre-specific note; follow the ratio's tip in README.md "Ratios".
